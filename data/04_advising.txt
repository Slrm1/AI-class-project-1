Source: Howard University Office of Undergraduate Studies — Academic Advising

For Computer Science students, Howard's Office of Undergraduate Studies lists Ladan Johnson as an advisor for last names A-K and Bobbi Harewood as an advisor for last names L-Z. The listed location is Carnegie, 2395 6th St. NW. The page also says students who cannot reach their faculty advisor during the summer can contact Dr. Ivory Berry, Assistant Dean, for additional assistance for Engineering and Architecture students.

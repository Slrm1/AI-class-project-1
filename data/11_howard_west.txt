A Howard Magazine article about Howard West describes students gaining real-world technology experience through an immersive program. Students worked on teams with Google employees and experienced project leadership, management, team roles, and deliverables. The article describes the experience as helping students understand the pressures of a technology career and preparing them for employment after graduation.

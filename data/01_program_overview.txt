Source: Howard University Programs — Bachelor of Science in Computer Science
URL: https://programs.howard.edu/bachelor-science-computer-science

Howard's Computer Science BS requires at least 120 credit hours. The program emphasizes solving real-world problems and prepares students for industry, graduate study, research, entrepreneurship, and technology leadership. Students can explore areas including AI, human-centered computing, cybersecurity, computer systems and networks, robotics, gaming, and computational science and mathematics. The program also highlights hands-on learning, research, and industry engagement.

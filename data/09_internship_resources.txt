Source: Howard University Office of Undergraduate Studies — Internship Resources
URL: https://ous.howard.edu/career-resources/internship-resources

Howard says thousands of companies post full-time jobs, internships, fellowships, and other opportunities directly to Handshake for Howard graduate and undergraduate students. The page also describes externships as short-term job-shadowing experiences and micro-internships as short-term, paid professional assignments. Micro-internships can occur year-round and typically range from 5 to 40 hours.

Source: Howard University Programs — Bachelor of Science in Computer Science
URL: https://programs.howard.edu/bachelor-science-computer-science

Howard describes the Computer Science bachelor's program as an undergraduate B.S. program. Students may explore focus areas including artificial intelligence, human-centered computing, cybersecurity, computer systems and networks, robotics, gaming, and computational science and mathematics. The program emphasizes hands-on learning, research, and industry-engaged experiences. Program documents listed by Howard include a 2026-2027 Computer Science Program Handbook and Plan of Study.

Source: Howard University Office of Undergraduate Studies — Career Planning
URL: https://ous.howard.edu/career-resources/career-planning

Howard's career planning resources include resume review and assistance, cover letter review and assistance, and LinkedIn profile review. The university recommends students use these services to prepare for internship and job applications. The page lists the Office of Undergraduate Studies at the Carnegie Building, 2nd Floor, 2395 Sixth St. NW, Washington, DC 20059.

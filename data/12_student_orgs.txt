Source: Howard University College of Engineering and Architecture — Student Organizations
URL: https://cea.howard.edu/resources/student-orgs

The CEA student organizations page includes Upsilon Pi Epsilon (UPE), an international honor society for computing sciences. Howard's Beta Chapter in Washington, DC was chartered at Howard in 2004 and membership is by invitation. The page also lists engineering-related organizations and provides examples of student communities connected to academic and professional interests.
